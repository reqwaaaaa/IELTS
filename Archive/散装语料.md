## 一、写作 Task 2

### 1. 立论与衔接

#### 开头与立场

> With the rapid development of modern society and urbanisation, increasing attention has been drawn to the highly controversial issue of whether **B**. While this issue remains controversial, I believe that **C**.

适用于利弊题的立场：

> I tend to believe that the benefits brought about by this trend outweigh its drawbacks.

适用于“双边讨论”或平衡结论：

> Neither blindly focusing on the problems nor completely embracing the trend is desirable.

> In a nutshell, what matters most is striking a balance between **A** and **B**.

**注意：**如果题目问的是 *positive or negative development*，结论不能写 *balance*。应明确表态：

> I believe that this trend is, overall, a positive / negative development, because ...

再在主体或结尾中作综合判断。

#### 让步、转折与结尾

| 功能 | 可用表达 |
| --- | --- |
| 承认对方观点 | *Admittedly, ...* / *It is understandable why ...* |
| 转向主论点 | *However, excessive concern about XXX may lead to unintended consequences.* |
| 总结利弊 | *As has been stated above, although XXX may pose certain challenges, its benefits outweigh its drawbacks.* |
| 凝练总结 | *As has been stated above, the N, albeit challenging, brings considerable benefits to XXX.* |

### 2. 通用表达与论证

#### 高频句式与词汇

- `XXX, albeit challenging, brings considerable benefits to XXX.`
- `In addition, sharing information can help people feel a stronger sense of connection to a community, as it satisfies their need for social interaction and recognition.`
- `under the circumstance that ...`
- `particularly when ...`
- `substantial` = `significant` = `considerable`
- `substantially` / `marginally` / `generally`
- `jump on the bandwagon`
- `traffic is so lucrative`
- `This can undermine individual autonomy.`
- `mitigate potential risks`

#### 审慎论证

- `This does not necessarily mean that ...`
- `X does not automatically lead to Y.`
- `X alone is not enough to guarantee Y.`
- `XXX should be encouraged when the potential benefits outweigh the predictable costs.`
- `Neither A nor B is inherently better; the more suitable environment depends on people's priorities, occupations and stages of life.`

#### 原因、结果与例证

- `I think people with different backgrounds, personalities and abilities can actually complement each other. Sometimes, this kind of collaboration can lead to results that neither person could have achieved alone.`
- `Algorithm-driven platforms may repeatedly expose users to biased or repetitive information, making it harder for them to form independent opinions.`
- `Small achievements can create a positive feedback loop, making people more confident and motivated.`

### 3. 低频主题语料

#### 文化、历史与艺术

- `Cultural heritage is not merely a reminder of the past; it is also an important source of collective identity.`  
  文化遗产不仅是对过去的提醒，也是集体身份认同的重要来源。
- `Cultural diversity exposes people to different values and ways of life, encouraging them to look beyond their own assumptions.`  
  文化多样性让人们接触不同的价值观和生活方式，鼓励他们超越自身原有的认知假设。
- `Historical documents, photographs and artefacts can be digitised and preserved for future generations.`  
  历史文献、照片和文物可以被数字化，以供后代保存和研究。
- `Technology should complement, rather than replace, careful historical research and critical thinking.`  
  科技应该补充而不是取代严谨的历史研究和批判性思维。
- `Turning traditional culture into a commercial product can generate revenue, but it may also encourage superficial representations of cultural identity.`  
  将传统文化转化为商业产品可以创造收入，但也可能导致对文化身份的表面化呈现。
- `History education should go beyond memorising dates and events.`  
  历史教育不应局限于记忆日期和事件。
- `However, the value of art and culture cannot be measured solely in terms of their immediate economic returns.`  
  艺术和文化的价值不能仅用短期经济回报衡量。

#### 健康、教育与家庭

- `Health is shaped not only by individual choices but also by the social and economic conditions in which people live.`  
  健康不仅取决于个人选择，也受到社会和经济环境的影响。
- `Schools can teach knowledge and social rules, whereas families often play a more direct role in shaping children's values and daily habits.`  
  学校可以传授知识和社会规则，而家庭往往更直接地影响孩子的价值观和日常习惯。
- `While strict rules can provide structure, excessive control may prevent children from developing independent judgement.`  
  严格的规则能够建立秩序，但过度控制可能妨碍孩子形成独立判断力。

#### 广告、旅游与环境

- `Advertising often appeals to people's emotions and insecurities rather than their actual needs.`  
  广告往往利用人们的情绪和不安全感，而非真正的需求。
- `Although tourism can generate income and create jobs, excessive tourism may place considerable pressure on local communities and the environment.`  
  旅游业能够创造收入和就业，但过度旅游可能给当地社区和环境带来巨大压力。
- `Human well-being depends heavily on the stability of the natural systems that provide food, clean water and other essential resources.`  
  人类福祉高度依赖于自然系统的稳定，而这些系统提供食物、清洁水源等基本资源。
- `Once people's basic needs are met, the relationship between material wealth and well-being becomes more complex.`  
  当基本需求得到满足后，物质财富与幸福感之间会变得更加复杂。

## 二、写作 Task 1

### 1. 折线图

#### 开头与概述

- `The line graph illustrates changes in A between X and Y.`
- `The line graph compares the number of X in several categories between X and Y.`
- `the percentage of people who + 动词（如 used）`
- `A recorded the highest figure, while B remained the lowest.`
- `The most notable change was the sharp increase in A.`
- `The XXX experienced fluctuations throughout the period but showed an overall upward trend.`
- `Most categories declined over the period, with A experiencing the most significant decrease.`

#### 分组思路

1. **快速变化 + 缓慢变化 / 波动**
   - `Looking first at the XXX that witnessed a dramatic upward trend, ...`
   - `Regarding the declining and fluctuating XXX, ...`
2. **两组走势相似 + 一组特殊**
   - `The figures for A and B showed broadly similar patterns, although there were some subtle differences.`
   - `By contrast, C followed the opposite trend.`
   - `The corresponding figures for A and B were ...`
3. **多条线：最高 / 最低 / 差距**
   - `A consistently recorded the highest figure throughout the period.`
   - `B and C were broadly similar.`
   - `D was significantly lower than the other figures.`
   - `A remained the most popular category, while D was the least common.`

#### 比较与趋势表达

| 情形 | 表达 |
| --- | --- |
| A 比其他项目高 | `A was significantly higher than the other figures.` |
| A 比 B 高 | `A was considerably higher than B.` |
| 高 X 个百分点 | `A was X percentage points higher than B.` |
| A、B 几乎相同 | `A and B were almost identical.` |
| 分别为 | `A and B recorded 30% and 20%, respectively.` |
| 对照 | `A stood at 40%, whereas B accounted for only 20%.` |
| 最高 / 最低 | `A consistently recorded the highest / lowest figure throughout the period.` |
| 最大 / 最小占比 | `A accounted for the largest / smallest proportion, at 51% / 2%.` |
| 第二大 | `This was followed by B, which represented 22%.` |
| 相反 / 相比 | `By contrast, ...` / `Relative to XXX, ...` |

- `A rose from 30% in 1920 to 90% in 1980, before increasing relatively steadily over the following twenty years and reaching 100% in 2000.`
- `It fell sharply to 20, after which it fluctuated slightly.`
- `eventually reaching ...` / `ultimately standing at ...`
- `A accounted for / represented / constituted X% of the total.`

**检查清单**：注意名词单位（`number` / `percentage` / `proportion` / `dollars` / `hours`）；过去数据用过去时，预测数据用将来时或 `predicted to`。先找极值，再找总体趋势，然后分类（相似 / 相反），最后选择起点、终点、最高或最低等关键数字。

### 2. 表格、柱状图与饼图

#### Table

- **按列读：**`The table compares the six networks in terms of their age, size and the number of people who use them each year.`
- **按行读：**`The table shows data about the underground rail networks in six major cities.`
- **横向比较**: 比较有孩子与无孩子家庭时，可用 `The same pattern can be seen for ...`
- 观察哪些项目在各国均为最大 / 最小，哪些国家的某项目最多 / 最少。
- 若涉及时间，可先按年代分组（较早的一组、较新的一组），再作组内比较。

#### Bar chart

- 排名时先看：哪个国家整体最高？哪个商品整体最高？哪些国家相近？
- `It is clear that British people spent significantly more money than people in the other three countries on all six goods.`
- `Of the six items, consumers spent the most money on photographic film.`
- 若涉及不同时间，在排名后补充变化趋势。

#### Pie chart

- `The pie charts compare the amount of electricity produced using five different sources of fuel in two countries over two separate years.`
- 若有不同时间点，先纵向比较总体变化（总量与结构），再横向比较各细分项目。
- `While the totals for both countries were similar, there were big differences in the fuel sources used.`
- 注意结构：最大 / 最小项，是否平均分配。
- `A accounted for 60%, twice the proportion of B and six times that of C.`

### 3. 地图与流程图

#### Map

- `The map shows the growth / changes of XXX.`
- `The first picture shows the layout of XXX, and the second shows some proposed changes to the XXX.`
- `The main difference between the two sites is that ...`
- `Overall, the most noticeable changes are that several new facilities were introduced, while some original areas / features were removed.`
- `In addition, the area experienced a transformation from a mainly [old function] place into a more [new function] one.`
- `Looking at the newly constructed areas, ...`
- `Regarding the areas that underwent major changes, ...`
- `A new facility was introduced in the area where XXX used to be located.`
- `XXX was demolished to make way for XXX.`
- `Despite these changes, XXX remained unchanged throughout the period.`
- `The XXX was extended towards the north to accommodate more users.`
- `swapped their original locations`
- `at the intersection of ...` / `along the road` / `adjacent to ...`
- `to the north of ...` / `in the southern area` / `in the peripheral area`

平面图需注意标注颜色与设施是否已开放，例如：

> There will also be a new room for special exhibitions. This room is shown in red on the existing plan and is not currently part of the gallery.

#### Process

- `The picture illustrates the way in which ...`
- `The diagram illustrates the process of how ...`
- `Overall, the whole process consists of several stages, beginning with [initial stage] and ending with [final result].`
- `The process can be broadly divided into two main phases, involving [phase 1] and [phase 2].`
- `Overall, the process is cyclical.`
- `Initially, A is ... Following this, B is ... Subsequently, C is ..., resulting in D.`
- `During the later stages, E is ..., followed by F. Once this stage has been completed, G occurs. Ultimately, the final product is produced.`

## 三、口语 Part 3

### 1. 回答框架

#### 直接表达观点

> From my perspective, **[观点]**. The main reason is that **[原因]**. For example, **[例子]**. Consequently, I think **[结论]**.

#### 让步后表态

> It is understandable why some people think so, but I would argue that **[你的观点]**. Admittedly, **[让步]**. For instance, **[例子]**. However, **[限制 / 反面]**. Overall, **[最终判断]**.

#### 条件型 / 平衡型回答

> There isn't really a one-size-fits-all answer to this question. On the one hand, ... On the other hand, ... So ultimately, it highly depends on the context.

#### 趋势型回答

> I'd say this is becoming increasingly **[important / common]**. One major reason is that **[社会变化]**. As a result, **[影响]**. For example, **[例子]**. In the long run, **[长期影响]**.

### 2. 观点句与通用表达

- `I'm broadly in favour of A, but I don't think its benefits should be considered in isolation from its potential drawbacks.`
- `Even if A is highly beneficial, excessive emphasis on it may come at the expense of B.`
- `Honestly, I think it highly depends on the person rather than the format of the information itself.`
- `Honestly, it really depends on whether we're talking about the past or the present.`
- `Honestly, I think it really depends on the person, because it's usually not relevant to you at all.`

#### “最重要的是”

- `Honestly, I think what matters most is whether the ad grabs your attention straight away, because almost every ad today is skippable.`
- `Honestly, I think the best thing teachers can do is design projects that AI can't fully do on its own.`
- `Honestly, I'd say people need to be organised at work all the time now, mainly because of AI.`
- `Honestly, I'd say the main purpose of punishment is to stop people from doing the wrong thing in the first place.`
- `Honestly, I'd say the most important thing is consistency, practising a little every single day rather than a lot sporadically.`

#### “最大的原因是”

- `Honestly, I think the biggest reason is the excitement, because no one knows the result in advance.`
- `Honestly, I think the main reason is that school simply can't compete with what kids watch on their phones.`
- `Honestly, I think the main reason is that a lot of teachers don't actually love teaching. They're only in the job for the paycheck, so they put in the least effort possible.`

#### 补充与转折

- `On the other hand, when people actually want to learn something, they can use social media to find useful and practical information.`
- `That being said, practising with a machine can leave you unmotivated. If that's the case, it's better to practise with real people instead.`
- `Honestly, I don't think everything needs to be perfectly prepared. Sometimes over-preparing actually stops you from ever getting started.`
- `What really matters is helping people find something they're genuinely passionate about.`

### 3. 可复用例子

#### 文化差异与社会规则

- **文化差异：**`Let me give you an easy example. At a big celebration like Chinese New Year, all the dishes are placed in the middle of the table, and everyone shares from the same plates. Nobody has their own separate meal. It shows how much we value being together as one big family. By contrast, in many Western countries, food is often served on individual plates, which fits better with their emphasis on independence and personal space.`
- **学校规则：**`But if the school bans phones during classes, there is nothing to distract him anymore. He gradually starts paying attention again, and his grades recover.`
- **常见校规：**`Honestly, the most common rule in schools in my country is that every student has to wear a uniform.`
- **法律与全球共识：**`That being said, a few laws come close to being accepted worldwide. Almost every country now punishes factories that cause serious pollution, because dirty air and water harm everyone.`

#### 网络、商业与 AI

- **网络曝光 / 道路冲突：**`Let me give you an easy example. Imagine a man gets into a fight on the road and punches another driver. Someone nearby films the whole thing on their phone, and within hours, the video goes viral. People quickly find out who he is and where he works, and his company fires him to protect its reputation.`
- **外卖为何成功：**`Let me give you an easy example. Think about food delivery apps. They became huge because they solved a real, everyday problem. After a long day at work, millions of people are too tired to cook or go out, but they still need to eat. The app solves that problem perfectly by bringing hot food straight to their door. Because it addresses something so many people struggle with, it spread incredibly fast.`

- **组织能力：**`Honestly, I'd say people need to be more organised at work nowadays, mainly because of AI. In the past, most workers had just one role, so their jobs were relatively simple. But now AI makes each worker far more productive, so companies may not need as many people. Instead, they expect one worker to take on several roles at once.`

#### 家庭、关系与时间

- **父母给孩子压力：**`Honestly, I think the main reason is that parents today are afraid their children will fall behind. They worry that if their children don't work hard enough, they may lose opportunities in the future.`
- **童年朋友失联：**`Honestly, I've lost touch with most of my childhood friends because those friendships were mainly based on living nearby rather than having much in common.`
- **时间不足：**`Honestly, I'd say the biggest difficulty is simply not having enough time. Learning a language itself is pretty easy these days, with all the free YouTube tutorials and AI apps available. The real problem is fitting it into a busy life.`
- **公司裁员：**`Imagine a company announces major layoffs, and one worker gets so angry that he writes a long post online, calling his boss greedy and saying the company only cares about profit.`

#### 媒体、消费与生活方式

- **短视频成瘾：**`Let me give you an easy example. Think about a student who is addicted to short videos. Without any rules, he keeps his phone under the desk and watches TikTok during class. He learns nothing, and his grades keep dropping.`
- **现场看球：**`Ticket prices don't bother them, so they happily go to the stadium to watch big matches in person, because being there in a huge cheering crowd is an experience that watching at home can never match.`
- **社交媒体娱乐：**`Honestly, I'd say most people in my country are hooked on useless entertainment videos. Instead of sitting there struggling through an awkward conversation, you can quietly scroll through your Instagram feed and look at the reels and pictures you're actually interested in. So your phone saves you from a lot of embarrassment.`
- **食物与现代生活：**`Delivery apps are probably one of the main reasons why people eat more ready-made food nowadays.` / `Honestly, I'd say the biggest difference is how processed our food has become. These days, a lot of what people eat is fast and ready-made.`
- **明星带货：**`So the celebrity can instantly turn their fame and public influence into massive sales for the brand.`
- **青少年与网红：**`Let me give you an easy example. A twelve-year-old girl might spend hours watching influencers' videos every day, so she may simply copy whatever they say or do, such as believing that she needs to control her weight excessively.`

#### 扩展回答

> Our values and ways of thinking are not always compatible, so we often live somewhat separately and spend more time with people who share similar interests and values. I usually communicate, collaborate and study with these like-minded people. Most of them are people I have met online, along with a few high-school classmates whom I am still in touch with. Being around these people allows me to become more confident and proficient in the way I approach problems.

**女性常看的内容：**

They tend to follow beauty and lifestyle vloggers, watching how someone does her makeup, chooses her clothes or keeps fit, because these videos are more relatable to their everyday lives. Of course, people use videos for many different purposes, including alleviating stress, studying and gathering information.

## 四、口语 Part 2

### 1. 主题收束段

#### 地点

> What I cherish most about this place is not necessarily its scenery, convenience or other physical features, but the memories and sense of belonging associated with it. Whenever I go there, I can temporarily step away from the hustle and bustle of modern life and simply enjoy the moment with people who matter to me. In that sense, the place has become more than just a physical location; it represents a part of my life that I genuinely want to preserve. It reminds me that, apart from ambition and productivity, people also need simple pleasures and meaningful relationships in their daily lives.

#### 经历

> Looking back, what impressed me most was not the experience itself, but the way it changed my perspective. It made me realise that things are often more complicated than they may appear at first glance, and that simply working harder is not always enough. Sometimes, what really matters is taking a step back, looking at the situation from a broader perspective and considering the underlying factors. Since then, I have become more open-minded and more willing to learn from both my successes and my mistakes.

#### 人物

> Looking back, what stayed with me most was not simply what he did for me, but the way he influenced the way I see things. He made me realise that the qualities that truly matter are not necessarily knowledge or achievements, but the ability to remain calm, deal with difficulties and see things from different perspectives. Since then, I have gradually become more patient and open-minded, and I have also become more willing to listen to others and reflect on my own decisions. In that sense, he has had a lasting influence on me, and I believe I will continue to carry what I learned from this experience into the future.

### 3. 个人故事素材

#### 1. 毕设、GPU 故障与教授

**适用题目**：一次困难经历、向他人求助、敬佩的老师、解决问题、学到的重要一课。

> At that time, I was working on my graduation project, which involved a relatively complicated algorithm using parallel processing. Because of the amount of computation, it put a fairly heavy workload on my GPU.
>
> At first, everything seemed to be working normally. However, after I ran the program several times, my computer started behaving strangely. The GPU became unstable, and the program would sometimes suddenly crash or stop responding.
>
> I was completely puzzled because I couldn't figure out what was actually causing the problem. At first, I assumed that the algorithm was simply too computationally intensive, so I tried to reduce the workload and change some parameters. However, nothing really worked. After making several random adjustments, I gradually realised that I was basically shooting in the dark rather than actually solving the problem.
>
> That's when I decided to turn to my professor for help. What impressed me was that he didn't immediately tell me the answer. Instead, he asked me to calm down and take a step back so that I could look at the bigger picture.
>
> We first checked the hardware and the operating system, after which we moved on to the software environment and the algorithm itself. By breaking the problem down into smaller and more manageable parts, we gradually ruled out different possibilities and compared the results under different settings.
>
> Eventually, we found that the problem was related to GPU driver compatibility, which had caused the graphics card to behave abnormally. My professor then pointed out that I shouldn't simply try to eliminate every hardware limitation. Rather, I should take those limitations into account when designing the algorithm.
>
> So I went back to the drawing board, modified the algorithm, reduced the computational workload and improved the way some parts were processed. Eventually, the program became much more stable, and I was able to finish my graduation project successfully.
>
> Looking back, what impressed me most wasn't simply how we fixed the bug, but how he taught me to deal with uncertainty. Whenever I run into a difficult problem now, I try to calm down, break it into smaller parts and look for the underlying cause instead of blaming luck or making random adjustments.

#### 2. 考研复试失利与调整

**适用题目**：一次失败、重要决定、挫折、改变想法、长期目标。

> At that time, I had spent a long time preparing for my postgraduate interview. I had relatively high expectations because I had performed quite well in the preliminary exams and the written test.
>
> I had prepared both theoretical knowledge and practical skills, including algorithms, software engineering, operating systems and computer networks. Because I had invested so much time and effort, I became increasingly worried about the interview. The night before the interview, I was extremely nervous. I kept checking the time and imagining different possible outcomes, so I could barely fall asleep. The next morning, I went to the interview venue after only a few hours of sleep.
>
> I tried my best during the interview, but unfortunately, I didn't get through. At first, I was deeply disappointed. I had put so much effort into preparing for it that I started questioning my own ability and even wondered whether I had chosen the wrong direction.
>
> However, after I had calmed down, I decided to take a step back and look at the situation more objectively. I gradually realised that the problem wasn't simply that I hadn't worked hard enough. Rather, I had become too focused on the final result, while spending too much time worrying about whether I would succeed.
>
> More importantly, I hadn't carefully examined whether my preparation strategy was actually effective. I discussed the experience with my professor, who encouraged me to break the situation down instead of simply blaming myself. I started looking at what had gone wrong, what I could improve, and what I actually wanted to achieve in the long run.
>
> That experience was a blessing in disguise, because it forced me to reconsider the way I approached important goals. As a result, I decided not to give up. I reconsidered my plans, adjusted my preparation strategy and became more willing to listen to advice and consider different possibilities.
>
> Looking back, I now realise that failure itself isn't necessarily the biggest problem. What matters is whether you can learn from it and adjust your strategy. I used to focus too much on whether I would succeed, but now I pay more attention to whether the things I'm doing are actually moving me towards my long-term goals.

#### 3. 外婆与春节包饺子

**适用题目**：难忘的家人、传统食物、童年回忆、珍贵物品 / 时刻。

> The person I associate most strongly with this memory is my grandmother. She has always been a very hands-on person. She used to grow vegetables herself and even raise chickens and ducks at home. >
> One of my strongest memories is making dumplings with her during the Spring Festival. We would prepare the vegetables and meat, make the dough, mix the filling and then wrap the dumplings together. When I was younger, I wasn't particularly good at wrapping them, so my dumplings often looked quite strange. However, she never made fun of me. She would simply smile and patiently show me how to do it properly.
>
> Sometimes we would sit together for quite a long time, talking about ordinary things while sharing little stories about my childhood. There was nothing particularly exciting about those moments, but somehow they became some of my most cherished memories.
>
> What made me appreciate those moments even more was that I later went to university in another province. After that, I could only go home about once a year, so I didn't have many opportunities to spend that kind of time with her. That's when I gradually realised that the value of those dumplings wasn't really about the food itself. It was about the time we spent together and the sense of connection to family and community.
>
> Looking back, I think that's why those ordinary moments have stayed with me for so long. Whenever I think about those dumplings, I actually think about my grandmother, my childhood and the simple sense of belonging that I had when I was young. In the future, I will still work hard and pursue my career, but I also want to leave enough room for the people and things that actually make life meaningful.

#### 4. 上海与松江：效率和生活的平衡

**适用题目**：喜欢的地方、一次旅行、城市与乡村、放松的经历、生活观念的改变。

> A few years ago, I spent quite a lot of time in Shanghai because I had some academic and career-related things to deal with there. Most of my time was around the city centre, where everything was extremely convenient and efficient. There were modern office buildings, shopping centres and excellent public transport, and people seemed to be constantly moving from one place to another.
>
> At first, I actually enjoyed that atmosphere. It gave me the feeling that everyone was working towards something. As a computer science student, I could relate to that mindset because I also tend to focus heavily on productivity and modernisation. However, after spending several days there, I started to notice another side of the city. Everything seemed to be measured by efficiency. People were always in a hurry, and even when they had some free time, they often used it to deal with work or other responsibilities.
>
> That's when I gradually realised that being productive all the time doesn't necessarily mean living a better life. Feeling a little overwhelmed, I went to Songjiang, on the outskirts of Shanghai, where my grandmother lived. The atmosphere there was completely different. Instead of skyscrapers and busy roads, there were small residential areas, rivers, trees and open spaces. My grandmother also grew some vegetables and kept chickens and ducks, so the whole place felt much more connected to ordinary daily life.
>
> Sometimes we would sit together for quite a long time, talking about ordinary things while sharing little stories about my childhood. There was nothing particularly exciting about those moments, but somehow they became some of my most cherished memories. At one point, we stopped by the river and just sat there for a while. There was no particular schedule, and nobody was asking us to be productive.
>
> What I found interesting was that I actually felt much more relaxed and mentally clear after that short visit. It made me think about the relationship between work and personal life in a different way. I still value ambition, efficiency and professional development. Nevertheless, I don't think people should sacrifice all their spare time for productivity. Whenever I go there, I can temporarily step away from the hustle and bustle of modern life and simply enjoy the moment with people who matter to me. In that sense, the place has become more than just a physical location; it represents a part of my life that I genuinely want to preserve. It reminds me that, apart from ambition and productivity, people also need simple pleasures and meaningful relationships in their daily lives.
