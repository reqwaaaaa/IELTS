## IELTS Writing Task 2｜语料库

### 1. 城市类
- People move to cities in search of job opportunities, and cities can offer greater employment possibilities and a higher standard of living.
- Traditional activities like farming need fewer workers nowadays, so many people migrate to cities from the countryside.
- City life can be convenient, but it is also more expensive, crowded, and stressful.
- One of the main problems in cities is traffic congestion, along with pollution, noise, and crime.
- There is often a wider gap between the rich and the poor in large cities.
- Governments should improve public transport, develop satellite towns, and invest in more affordable housing.
- More green spaces and better public facilities would make a city far more liveable.
- In short, urban life needs to be made more balanced rather than simply faster.

### 2. 传统习俗类
- Most products are now made in factories, and machinery has replaced skilled human labour.
- Goods are produced very quickly and in large numbers, which makes them cheaper and more accessible.
- However, factory work is often repetitive and leaves people feeling unfulfilled.
- Modern development has changed the way people live, but it has not necessarily improved every aspect of life.
- Traditional customs are still important during weddings and religious festivals.
- People often wear traditional costumes and eat special foods on such occasions.
- It is important to maintain our different cultural identities.
- We should celebrate festivals, teach traditional skills, and protect historic places.
- The key is not to reject modernity, but to protect what still gives society a sense of continuity.

### 3. 犯罪类
- The police should be in close contact with schools and focus on young people who have dropped out of school.
- These teenagers may become involved with gangs if they are left without support.
- Many prisoners re-offend when they are released, especially if they have a criminal record.
- A criminal record makes finding a job much more difficult.
- Community service can be more constructive than simply imprisoning offenders.
- Offenders can repay their community by cleaning streets or talking to school groups.
- The mass media often focus on violent and sensational crimes, which can lead to fear of crime among the public.
- A more effective system should combine punishment, rehabilitation, and prevention.

### 4. 环境类
- Factories and vehicles produce emissions and exhaust fumes.
- Cheap air travel is allowing more people to fly, which increases carbon emissions.
- Human activity is a major factor in the rise of greenhouse gases.
- We are destroying wildlife habitats and cutting down enormous areas of rainforest.
- This has led to the extinction of many species of flora and fauna.
- Governments could introduce laws to limit emissions from factories.
- They should invest in renewable energy such as solar, wind, or water power.
- They could also impose green taxes on drivers and airline companies.
- Government campaigns should promote recycling and lower consumption.
- Climate change cannot be solved by a magic bullet; it requires sustained and coordinated action.

### 5. 工作类
- The main reason many people go to work is to earn money.
- People need to pay bills, buy food, and cover everyday expenses.
- They also need to look after their families and save for the future.
- Employees with a stable career often have a good pension and health insurance.
- Their salaries may gradually increase over time, and they may be promoted within the organization.
- A clearly defined career path can give people a strong sense of security.
- People who change jobs can gain experience and develop a wider range of skills.
- Self-employment offers greater freedom, but it also involves financial risks.
- Many new businesses fail to make a profit.
- Work is not only about money; it also gives people identity, purpose, and job satisfaction.
- A good work-life balance can raise job satisfaction and prevent burnout.

### 6. 教育类
- Education gives people knowledge and practical skills.
- People with qualifications are more likely to find work and earn a higher salary.
- Education helps people contribute positively to society.
- Schools should also teach moral values such as responsibility, cooperation, and respect.
- Schools prepare children to become members of society.
- Overseas students are exposed to different cultures and customs, and they will learn a foreign language.
- Studying in an unfamiliar culture can be challenging, and the language barrier can be a serious problem.
- Living alone in a foreign environment can also cause homesickness.
- At the same time, it can make people more independent and resilient.
- Technology can enhance a teacher’s lessons, and students can use online resources to help with homework.
- Modern LLMs can help students with long-term academic planning and short-term goals.
- A classroom should be judged by engagement as well as exam results.
- Educational progress is usually incremental rather than dramatic.
- There are no magic bullets in education; support matters more than slogans.

### 7. 科技、网络、电视、报纸类
- Newspapers and books are now published online as well as in print.
- We can read the news from any country in the world.
- It costs almost nothing to publish or access information on the Internet.
- Mobile phones have revolutionized the way we communicate.
- We can stay in touch with family, friends, and colleagues wherever we are.
- With so many websites, it is often difficult to search for good information.
- Many websites contain offensive content, and some sites show violent or sexual images.
- Large language models have lowered the barrier to entry for information retrieval and drafting.
- LLM outputs should be treated as a starting point rather than a final authority.
- They can be fluent and persuasive, yet still fundamentally inaccurate.
- Users should cross-check key claims against reliable sources.
- AI agents are especially useful when a task involves multiple steps.
- They can break vague goals into smaller actions and keep moving towards the end goal.
- The real value lies in human-AI collaboration, not blind automation.
- In many online forums, the signal-to-noise ratio is uneven.
- Some threads are genuinely insightful, while others are dominated by recycled opinions and emotional reactions.
- Online forums can become echo chambers.
- The internet makes knowledge more accessible, but it also makes misinformation easier to spread.
- Critical thinking matters more than simple access to information.
- Traditional media no longer monopolize information; they now compete with short videos, forums, and AI-generated summaries.

### 8. 通用论证句
- Correlation does not equal causation.
- Anecdotal evidence is not enough.
- It is not necessarily the enemy.
- This is not an easy fix.
- It is more of a game of inches than a game of miles.
- The evidence remains sparse.
- A classic case in point is ...
- While it is true that ..., ...
- On the flip side, we cannot ignore ...
- The bottom line is that it depends on the context.
- In short, it is a trade-off.
- A more realistic approach would be to ...
- People should take concrete steps to ...
- This can go a long way towards ...
- It can move the needle on the outcome.

### 9. 可直接收口的结尾句
- The key is to stay realistic rather than radical.
- What matters most is balance, not extremism.
- The best solution is usually gradual, practical, and evidence-based.
- In the end, the issue is not whether something is perfect, but whether it is genuinely useful.
