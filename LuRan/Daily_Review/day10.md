* 第四章：Part2技巧
  > 按照主题分类

  一. **人物篇**(teacher, famous person, friend, family member, child, someone who helps people, someone who does something well)
  - 1、**思考**：`Who (appearance, personality) ?`, `What he/she does`, `When/how/where you met`, `What do you know about this person`, `Why you like this person?`
  - 2、回答**思路**
    ```
    ① 先让画面出现（appearance && how you met） - 人物外貌、场景、行为。
    ② 再让人物立住（characteristic） - 聪明、独特、思想。
    ③ 然后回到自己（relationship） - 我与此人的互动，我在影响下做了什么、如何变化。
    ④ 最后一句收情绪 - 总结这个人对自己的影响，为何喜欢。  
    ``` 

  二. **地点篇**（a city (you've visited/you would like to visit), somewhere you went on holiday, a historic place, a river, lake or sea, a journey (where you went), a shop, a restaurant）
  - 1、常用**词汇**
    - 1.**interesting/fun city**:`lively`, `bustling`, `hectic`, `thriving`, `multicultural`, `fascinating`, `unique`, `a special atmosphere`, `entertainment`, `an unforgettable experience`, `the time flew by`,`endless things to see and do`
    - 2.**historic**:`ancient`, `monuments`, `landmarks`, `where great events took place`, `connection to the past`, `reminders of the past`, `everywhere you look`, `full of history`, `a rich cultural heritage`
    - 3.**friendly(restaurant)**:`welcoming`, `approachable`, `polite`, `thoughtful`, `attentive`, `great atmosphere`, `make you feel at home`, `the service was excellent`, `nothing was too much to ask`
  - 2、回答**思路**
    ```
    ① 先让画面出现 - 地点名称、位置。
    ② 再描述地点 - 名气、特点、所在城市背景。
    ③ 然后回到自己（relationship） - 我与此地的关系，举例感叹自己的游览、体验、感受。
    ④ 最后一句收情绪 - 总结自己的喜欢/讨厌情绪以及一些原因。  
    ``` 
  
  三. **物品篇**（something you own, something you would like to own, something you use every day, a piece of electronic equipment, a gift you received, a gift you gave, something old that you own）
  - 1、**思考**：`What the object is?`, `Where you got it from?`, `Who gave it to you?`, `How long you have this object?`, `Why do you like this object?`, `Why do you think it's special?`, `Why do you want it?`
  - 2、回答**思路**
    ```
    ① 先让画面出现 - 什么物品、物品外表/材质、来源。
    ② 再描述物品 - 使用方式/场景、价值。
    ③ 然后回到自己（relationship） - 我与此物的互动、渊源，喜欢/讨厌这个东西的理由。
    ``` 

  四. **事件篇**（an important event in your life, a festival in your country, a party, a celebration, a sports event that you watched）
  - 1、**思考**：`What the event was?`, `What type of the event was?`, `Where it was?`, `Who went?`, `Who was performing?`, `Who was in the event?`, `Why did you like it?`, `Why did you enjoy the event?`, `Why do people enjoy this event or this kind of event?`
  - 2、回答**思路**
    ```
    ① 先让画面出现 - 什么事情、发生时间、地点、涉及人物。
    ② 具体事件类型 - 事件背景（结婚/节日）烘托气氛，描述场景。
    ③ 然后回到自己（relationship） - 我如何参与了这个事件，自己的感受。
    ④ 最后一句收情绪 - 回味/推荐/展望。  
    ``` 
  - 3、常用**词汇**
    - 1.**a 'special' event**:`memorable`, `unforgettable`, `spectacular`, `fantastic`, `it was a once-in-a-lifetime experience`, `I enjoyed every minute`, `I was blown away`, `it exceeded my expectations`, `an unforgettable experience`
    - 2.**where the event was:**:`a perfect /prime location`, `beautifully decorated`, `romantic`, `the atmosphere was eletric`, `service, staff, attention to detail`

  五. **活动篇**（a hobby, a sport, a game, something healthy that you do, something new that you have do）
  - 1、**思考**：`What the activity is?`, `Where you do it?`, `Who you do it with?`, `Why you like it?`
  - 2、回答**思路**
    ```
    ① 先让画面出现 - 什么活动、活动的背景，好处，参与的理由。
    ② 进入这个活动 - 自己何时/如何/为何参与这个活动。
    ③ 然后回到自己（relationship） - 参与这个活动的细节，因此获得的心理/思想/身体/技能提升。
    ④ 最后一句收情绪 - 赞扬这个活动，表达快乐积极的情绪。  
    ``` 
  - 3、常用**词汇**
    - 1.**a healthy sport**:`stay in good condition`, `get in better shape`, `feel refreshed`, `rejuvenated`, `invigorated`, `gives me an energy boost`, `a good cardiovascular workout`, `build strength and endurance`
    - 2.**an enjoyable game**:`hone my skills`, `thinking ahead`, `out-think/outwit the opponent`, `concentration, mental workout`, `learn from mistakes/losses`

  六. **喜好篇**（film or TV programme, song, piece of music, book, website, advertisement）
  - 1、**思考**：`What is your favorite book?`, `When did you first read it?` `Where did you find this book`, `How long have you had this book`, `Why do you like it?`
  - 2、回答**思路**
    ```
    ① 先让画面出现 - 喜好是什么，自己怎么接触到这个喜好的。
    ② 再让喜好立住 - 这个喜好的好处/优点/特征。
    ③ 然后回到自己（relationship） - 自己如何执行这个喜好，从中获得了什么益处，思想/能力的提升。
    ④ 最后一句收情绪 - 喜欢的理由，赞美，后续期待。  
    ``` 
  - 3、常用**词汇**
    - 1.**advertisement**:`synchronise with phone/computer`, `contacts, photos, emails`, `store music`, `receive notifications`, `search the web`, `it responds to voice commands`, `track exercise goals`, `fashion accessory-it looks stunning`
    - 2.**song**:`theme music`, `orchestra`, `instruments`, `well-known`, `immediately recognisable`, `creates expectation`, `adventure, mystery, suspense`
    - 3.**film**:`the plot (story)`, `the genre e.g. action, drama`, `the cast: actors, roles, characters scenes, locations` `special effects`, `I thoroughly enjoyed it`, `I was on the edge of my seet`
    - 4.**book**:`storyline, plot, genre, field, subject`, `the author, the characters`, `entertaining, enthralling,informative, fascinating, inspiring`, `I couldn't put it down`, `I learnt about /I found out about`, `it opened my mind to ...`

* 第五章：Part3技巧
  > 按照主题分类
  
  > 与Part1只讲4句话不同，Part3需要**点出核心论点/观念并进行举例论证和总结**，是一个完整的句段

  一. **对比**类型(性别、古今、年龄、地域)
  - 1、导入语 + 主旨句：`Well obviously. There are a range of potential distinctions here.`
  - 2、转折：`Whereas on the other hand`, `On the contrary`, `Though quite the opposite`
  - 3、本质是提出**两方各自现象+背后原因**
  
  二. **预测**类话题(... be like in the future?)
  - 1、导入语 + 主旨句：`Certainly, I'm sure that ...`, `I reckon that in the not so distant future`
  - 2、将来进行时：`By next century, some say that we will perhaps be living on other planets.`, `will be doing`
  - 3、将来完成时（将来某个节点已经完成了**have been done sth by**）：`I will have eaten my dinner by 8 p.m.`
  - 4、本质是提出**想象+展望+情绪（消/积极）**

  三. **优缺点**类话题(What are the advantages and disadvantages of ...?)
  - 1、**同意替换**：
    - Advantages:`Merit`, `Positive aspects`, `Favorable features`, `Bonus`, `Plus point`
    - Disadvantages:`Drawbacks`, `Shortcomings`, `Unfavorable aspects`, `Adverse features`, `Handicaps`
  - 2、主旨句：`Obviously there are a number of obvious merits`, `Of course there are a couple of shortcomings.`, `This is usually an aggravation because ...`, `I suppose the most unfavorable quality might be that ...`
  - 3、本质是提出**几个优/缺点+原因**要客观不要情绪

  四. **个人意见**类话题(Do you think/feel/opinion?)
  - 1、常用**词汇**:`I believe/guess/suppose ...`, `From my personal perspective/from where I stand`, `Definitely/certainly`, `I partially agree`
  - 2、主旨句：`Can l just say very adj? because that's the first word popping into my head for me, its kind of given that ...`, `This may sound reasonable, but I still think its vital to recognize the importance of ...`
  - 3、本质是提出**自我见解+举例**

  五. **原因**类话题(Why ...? / What cause ...?)
  - 1、常用**词汇**:
    - 表原因:`That's due to the fact that…`, `This is mainly because…`, `The key factor would be that…`, `One primary/fundamental/critical reason for… is…`
    - Quantities of people:`Almost everybody`, `The majority of xxx people`
    - Age:`Elderly people/retired people/the older generation/pensioner/senior citizen Working people/office workers`
  - 2、主旨句：`There are obviously a number of motives surrounding this idea.`
  - 3、本质是提出**现象+原因+解决方法**

  六. **后果**类话题(What effects ...?)
  - 1、常用**词汇**:`Lead to…`, `Result in…`, `Cause…`, `Consequently…`, `As a result…`, `Therefore…`
  - 2、主旨句：`It's universally accepted that there are a few hazards involved with…`, `Essentially, one fundamental concern is probably that…`
  - 3、本质是提出**结果+原因+改善建议**

  七. **解决措施**类话题(How/What do you think/suggest we can solve ...)
  - 1、常用**词汇**:`Should pay more attention to...`, `Ought to make an effort to...`, `Can provide more...`, `Could try harder to...`, `Should take measures to...`, `Ought to take steps to...`
  - 2、主旨句：`I honestly believe that there are a few ways to tackle these `
  - 3、本质是提出**看法+解决方法+执行建议**

  八. **变化**类话题(How have people's attitudes towards travelling changed in recent years?)
  - 1、导入语 + 主旨句：`The way people ... has changed dramatically.`
  - 2、本质是提出**对比（变化）+好处+看法**
