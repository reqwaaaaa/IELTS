### 外刊精读
> **Is bone broth good for you? Or is it just tasty**

> **keyWords**: bone broth \ wellness industry \ evidence-based nutrition

#### 核心判断
这篇文章是典型的“养生产品证伪”型写法：先承认骨汤在减肥、胶原、关节等方面有一定 **plausible** 逻辑，再逐段指出证据不足、研究设计有限、因果难以成立，最后落到一个克制结论——它更像“有安慰感的饮品”，而不是被神化的健康灵药。

#### 有意思的句子用法
- `the evidence ... is almost as thin as the drink itself`：既说证据薄，也呼应骨汤“稀薄”。
- `generally contradicted the popular narrative`：适合写“研究结果与流行说法相反”。
- `Whether a similar process is at play in humans will take time to investigate.`：科学写作里很常见的保留表达，表达“不下结论”。
- `the only harm bone broth will do is to your wallet`
- `In short, although ... evidence remains sparse.`：适合做评论文总结句。

#### 写作积累
1. **Health / Wellness**
- `Wellness products should be judged by evidence, not hype.`
- `A product may sound healthy, but that does not prove real benefits.`
- `Some claimed effects are plausible, yet still unproven.`
- `A comforting habit is not the same as a medically effective one.`

2. **Science / Evidence**
- `A lack of a control group weakens causal claims.`
- `Correlation alone is not enough to prove effectiveness.`
- `Results from animal studies do not automatically apply to humans.`
- `Evidence may be suggestive, but it remains sparse.`
