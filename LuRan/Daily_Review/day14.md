### 口语
> 真题训练

* 第九章：Part3真题
  > 三节

  一. **预测类**
  - 1、叠甲：`That's a really abstract question,not gonna lie, it's not something I'd normally sit around thinking about.`
  - 2、`On the one hand, xxx.`, `Whereas on the other hand, xxx.`, `Overall,it can be said that` (**从三个角度对比，并总结**)
  - 3、`Initially, I guess that we might begin to see...`, `Simultaneously, I suppose it's quite probable that we may have...`, `And who knows, it could even be possible that we'll see the arrival of...`(**这个模版用于较确定的话题，核心不在比较而是阐述**)
  - 4、**拿到题下意识形成相反的思考角度**：*Will small markets disappear in the futur?* 思考：`1.challenge to small markets`, `2.small markets have their unique appeal`
  - 5、**现有的社会事实**：*Do you think people will feel even more tired in the future?* 思考：`1. stress level increase`, `technology may solve problems`
  - 6、**往下垂直细分主题**：水果 - 布拿拿

  二. **优缺点类**
  - 1、开头直接明确观点：`There is no doubt that...`
  - 2、第一点`Firstly...`, 第一点的理由`This is obviously favorable because...`, 第二点以及理由`So that's one thing, then again, I guess...`, 总结`In a nutshell`
  - 3、**拿到题下意识思考主题特有的属性、效率、安全、收益几个角度**
    - e.g. *What are the benefits of elderly people live alone?* 思考：`1.boost self-esteem`, `2.peaceful environment`, `3.reduce conflicts and misunderstandings`

  三. **个人意见类**
  - 1、是否必要/重要型：`Can l just say very important? because that's the first word popping into my head for me, its kind of given that + 题目改写.`, `Of course some people might disagree, they might think, and they might think its because...`, `First of all, I guess it's vital for every individual. Take me as an example, I think + 个人原因.`, `Also, I think there will also be some societal benefits. Just imagine + 社会原因.`, `So, to sum up, from the two perspectives, both individual and societal, I think it is important to acknowledge that + 题目改写.` 
  - 2、对某事的看法/做法型：`Yeah. There is no doubt that + 题目改写.`, `First of all, the fact that...`, `Take the case of..., a typical change among thousands, which can...`, `As a result, it's evident that 结论/好处.`
  - 3、e.g. `What advice should young people follow?`
    - 年轻人可以听各种建议。
    - 但前提是建议来自“好意”，不是套路或欺骗。
    - 最后决策必须是自己的。
    - 否则遇到问题就会怪别人，这不成熟。
  - **考虑一个行为最直接的后果，释放的深层信号如性格，行事方式，以及这样做的直接好/坏处，此外经济、长远影响、健康等因素也可以考虑**
