* 第九章：Part3真题
  > 三节

  一. **原因类**
  - 1、多元视角看待原因：`That's a tough question, there's no one-size-fits-all answer. I think it really depends on the context, like whether we're looking at it from an individua's perspective or a company's, or even society as a whole.`, `Firstly, I guess it has something to do with society as a whole. I mean, (社会原因1). So when people (动作+结果).`, `Correspondingly, if we are looking for more reasons, I guess it might have something to do with the (经济/技术/个人情感).`, `To sum up, there are a lot of reasons behind this phenomenon, but I guess I just mentioned the most important two or three reasons.`
  - 2、**下意识思考社会背景原因（societal norms）和自身成长、教育、性格因素（symbol of）**
    - e.g. expensive goods：`societal symbol`:status、success; `marketing strategies`; `unique design, quality`
  - 3、e.g. `Why is punctuality important to people?`
    - 守时 = 尊重别人的时间。（行为）
    - 守时 = 说明你能掌控自己的安排。（能力、性格）
    - 守时 = 有条理、可靠。（symbol）
    - 所以大家重视守时。（社会共识）
  
  二. **后果类**
  - 1、直接说后果可能不好/有好有坏：`Obviously we can say there are quite a lot of dangers with regard to this issue...`, `has its pros and cons`, `At the outset, the most crucial predicament is that…`, `Additionally, another major cause for concern has to be the fact that…`, `To wrap it up, like most things,it's a trade-off. There are good points on both sides, They make life easier`
  - 2、**思考对社会、个人生活、工作与发展的影响**比如旅游的话题就需要考虑*税收（旅馆、纪念品、餐厅）；当地居民就业（餐厅、公共设施）；个人的多元文化交流；以及附带的坏处（城市喧闹，交通压力）*
  
  三. **解决措施类**
  - 1、`There is no doubt that ... play a crucial role in ...`, `Firstly, ... contribute to ... For instance, ... which is essential for ...`, `So that's one thing, and as well as this, I guess ... could also have a positive effect on ... They offer access to ... which is particularly beneficial for ...`, `Lastly, ... can also have a significant ...`
  - 2、**思考对于社会共识而言的做法、适合自己的做法、对自己/群体有利的做法，总结益处**
  - 3、e.g. `What can a teacher do to help his or her students become more confident?`
    - 1.supportive classroom environmen
    - 2.provide constructive feedback and suggestions
