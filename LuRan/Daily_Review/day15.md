### 口语

* 第九章：Part3真题
  > 三节

  一. **原因类**
  - 1、多元视角看待原因：`That's a tough question, there's no one-size-fits-all answer. I think it really depends on the context, like whether we're looking at it from an individua's perspective or a company's, or even society as a whole.`, `Firstly, I guess it has something to do with society as a whole. I mean, (社会原因1). So when people (动作+结果).`, `Correspondingly, if we are looking for more reasons, I guess it might have something to do with the (经济/技术/个人情感).`, `To sum up, there are a lot of reasons behind this phenomenon, but I guess I just mentioned the most important two or three reasons.`
  - 2、**下意识思考社会背景原因（societal norms）和自身成长、教育、性格因素（symbol of）**
    - e.g. *expensive goods*：`societal symbol`:status、success; `marketing strategies`; `unique design, quality`
  - 3、e.g. *Why is punctuality important to people?*
    - 守时 = 尊重别人的时间。（行为）
    - 守时 = 说明你能掌控自己的安排。（能力、性格）
    - 守时 = 有条理、可靠。（symbol）
    - 所以大家重视守时。（社会共识）
  
  二. **后果类**
  - 1、直接说后果可能不好/有好有坏：`Obviously we can say there are quite a lot of dangers with regard to this issue...`, `has its pros and cons`, `At the outset, the most crucial predicament is that…`, `Additionally, another major cause for concern has to be the fact that…`, `To wrap it up, like most things,it's a trade-off. There are good points on both sides, They make life easier`
  - 2、**思考对社会、个人生活、工作与发展的影响**比如旅游的话题就需要考虑*税收（旅馆、纪念品、餐厅）；当地居民就业（餐厅、公共设施）；个人的多元文化交流；以及附带的坏处（城市喧闹，交通压力）*
  
  三. **解决措施类**
  - 1、`There is no doubt that ... play a crucial role in ...`, `Firstly, ... contribute to ... For instance, ... which is essential for ...`, `So that's one thing, and as well as this, I guess ... could also have a positive effect on ... They offer access to ... which is particularly beneficial for ...`, `Lastly, ... can also have a significant ...`
  - 2、**思考对于社会共识而言的做法、适合自己的做法、对自己/群体有利的做法，总结益处**
  - 3、e.g. *What can a teacher do to help his or her students become more confident?*
    - 1.supportive classroom environmen
    - 2.provide constructive feedback and suggestions

### 作文
> 真题训练

* 第四章：大作文真题
  > 三节

  一. **城市类**
  - 1、***Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages？*** 此类题一边写一段， 在开头段和总结段亮明自己的观点
  - 2、e.g. *More and more people are migrating to cities in search of a better life, but city life can be extremely difficult. Explain some of the difficulties of living in a city. How can governments make urban life better for everyone?*
    - 开头段：引出主题，给出一般性答案。
    - 主体段：陈述难点：生活成本高，失业率高，有压力，交通拥堵，污染（谈问题）主体段：公租房，开发卫星城，促进郊区经济发展，提升公共交通，减少汽车（谈解决措施）。
    - 总结段：重申（改写）观点。
  - 3、**思考城市界面（交通、公共设施、环境）；居民生活（物价、人文、经济收入）；解决方法**
  - 4、**语料库**：
    - `People move to cities in search of job opportunities. Cities can offer greater employment possibilities and a higher standard of living.`, `Traditional activities like farming need fewer workers nowadays. People migrate to cities from the countryside.`
    - `Some people do not manage to find work. Homelessness and poverty are common in the cities. There is a gap between the rich and the poor.` 
    - `Life in the cities can be extremely stressful.`
    - `There are problems like traffic congestion and crime in the cities.`

  二. **传统习俗类**
  - 1、e.g. *People nowadays work hard to buy more things. This has made our lives generally more comfortable, but many traditional values and customs have been lost and this is a pity. To what extent do you agree or disagree?*
    - （1）购买东西让我们的生活更加美好（buying things has made our lives morecomfortable）
    - （2）传统的价值观念和习俗消失殆尽（traditional values and customs have been lost）
    - 如上挖掘出两个**观点**，同意这两个观点，则两个主体段就分别**论述（论据、解释、举例）**两个观点
  - 2、**思考现代发展（互联网、快递、交通、原子化）；传统（做法和观念和社区归属感）**
  - 3、**如果要写折衷观点，一定要表达中立，不要模棱两可和偏心** e.g. `有没有哪些传统文化消失了但另一些却保留了？科技是摧毁传统文化的唯一力量吗？难道我们要忽略现代科技对传统文化的影响吗？`
  - 4、**语料库**：
    - `Most products are now made in factories. Machinery has replaced skilled human labour. Goods are produced very quickly and in large numbers. Products are cheaper, which means that more people can buy them. Factory work is boring and leaves people feeling unfulfilled.`
    - `Traditional customs are still important during weddings and religious festivals. People wear traditional costumes and eat special foods.` 
    - `It is important to maintain our different cultural identities. We should celebrate festivals, teach traditional skills and protect historic places.`

  三. **科技、网络、电视、报纸类**
  - 1、**语料库**：
    - `Newspapers and books are now published online as well as in print. We can read the news in any language from any country in the world. It costs nothing to publish or access information on the Internet.`
    - `Mobile phones have revolutionized the way we communicate. We can stay in touch with family, friends and colleagues wherever we are.`
    - `With so many websites it is difficult to search for good information. Many websites contain offensive content. Some sites show violent or sexual images.`
